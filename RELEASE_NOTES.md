# Legs Mod v1.0.0

Give your Gorilla Tag arms a fun, leg-like look! Drop `LegsMod.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins` and hop in.

The mod adds the leg-like visuals, recognizes compatible modded players, and restores the normal hand pose near the computer and wardrobe.

Multiplayer visibility is still being tested. Both players need a compatible build, and a second modded client is needed to confirm what others see.
