# Legs Mod v1.0.9

Major fix: the long-standing hand-transform issue from the mod's first version is fixed. With Legs Mod enabled, the hands now stay at the wrist position they have when the mod is off.

Includes Toggle and Always On builds, with standard and Utilla options. Utilla builds require Utilla 1.5.0 or newer and only work in modded gamemodes.

The multiplayer markers remain compatible with version 1.0.0.
